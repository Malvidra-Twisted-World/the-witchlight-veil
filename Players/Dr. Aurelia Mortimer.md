> Dr. Aurelia Mortimer is an Edwardian physician who believes death is not fate, but miscalculation. During her early medical training, she witnessed entire neighborhoods fall to an unknown plague—patients dying in precise intervals, symptoms repeating with unnatural consistency, and no explanation that medicine or faith could provide.
> 
> Unable to accept death as coincidence or divine will, Aurelia devoted her life to studying not only medicine, but time itself.
> 
> Through forbidden research and a clockwork device of her own design, Aurelia touched the cosmic order that governs life and death. She learned to delay fate, predict endings, and correct outcomes—yet every change demands balance.
> 
> Now, Dr. Aurelia Mortimer travels as both physician and oracle. She still heals. She still saves lives. But she no longer asks whether she should interfere—only whether the price is one she is willing to pay.
> 
> She does not pray.
> She does not repent.
> 
> She listens to the ticking beneath reality, knowing that one day, inevitably, the mechanism will demand her.
> And when it does, Aurelia intends to understand it completely.
