A heir to royal throne but 74th layer down to succession. His awkward position couple up with no talent in military nor politic makes him earn no place in royal circle. So to forget his loneliness Lorelei spend his day reading books where he imagine himself as romance lead.

One day a strange carnival come to the city, and so Lorelei and his friends decided to sneak inside the carnival to see the wonder inside. There when lorelei see her, an otherworldly beautiful woman, autumn colored skin, eyes as green as emerald, hair beautiful curls of red, and heart as shinny as gold, he fall for her instantly. Althought their meeting cut short by carnival's hand, it sparks inspiration in lorelei's heart as it translated to beautiful poetry and song. Open up Lorelei's door to becoming bard with her as muse. And thus he rebel against his family wish as he set out to travel as artist.

Now years later he heard news that the carnival will come back to city, so he back home to once again enter the carnival to meet her.
