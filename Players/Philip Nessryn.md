> **Childhood beneath the lantern light**
> 
> As children, Philip Nessryn and his friends shared a forbidden joy: secretly entering the Witchlight Carnival. Slipping past guards and barriers became a game, and Philip quickly learned that clever misdirection and small illusions were far more effective than boldness. While others laughed loudly, Philip watched, planned, and guided events from the shadows, delighting in subtle chaos..
> 
> **The Night Everything When Wrong**
> 
> One fateful night, their luck ran out. The children were discovered and forcefully expelled from the carnival grounds. As the lights dimmed behind them and the music faded, none of them realized that the carnival took more than it gave. For Philip, something unseen but vital was stolen away in that moment.
> 
> ** The Slumbering Desire of a Trickster**
> 
> Shaken by his loss, Philip retreated into safety, choosing a life as a librarian and scribe. Surrounded by books and quiet order, he avoided the uncertainty of the road. Even so, his flaw followed him—he often vanished into his work, wandering the library in search of a desk he had left moments before.
> 
> To the world, Philip appears polite, reserved, and refined—a proper gentleman scholar. Yet beneath this calm surface sleeps the trickster’s heart he has never truly buried. A hunger for clever deception, playful misdirection, and subtle chaos stirs within him, restrained only by caution and fear.
