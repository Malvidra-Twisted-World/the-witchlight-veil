> Kee Chow Ma belonged to one of the knightly orders of the Greenfield Kingdom. His life was safe and secure, but everything changed when he was given a mission to investigate a forest in the south of the kingdom.
> 
> He wasn't alone, but with other warriors. When they arrived at the forest, they suddenly seemed to have moved dimensions or been trapped in a dark and eerie portal. All of Kee Chow Ma's comrades died, leaving him alone.
> 
> Just as he was about to give up, a sword approached him and said, "Grab me, and a contract will be made, and I will save you." Hearing this, Kee Chow Ma unhesitatingly grabbed the sword and fell unconscious.
> 
> When he regained consciousness, he was back at the forest entrance and saw the bodies of his comrades sucked into the sword he was holding. From that moment on, he was bound by a contract with a sword that sought souls to fuel its power. He could even sometimes hear the voices of the souls absorbed by the sword. Since that incident, Kee Chow Ma left the knightly order and became an adventurer or mercenary.
