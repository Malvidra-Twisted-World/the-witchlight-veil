# DM Preparation: Session 2

## Pre-Session Checklist
- [ ] **Trackers Ready:** Prepare a d8 for the **Time Track** and a token/die for the **Carnival Mood**.
- [ ] **Hour 1 Mechanics:** Be ready for the sycamore seed catch (DC 10 Dex) and the dandelion wish.
- [ ] **Treaclewise Rhyming Slang:** 
    *   *Toil and trouble* (Bubble)
    *   *Gnarly tree knot* (Teapot)
    *   *Bramble thicket* (Ticket)
    *   *Ready for a bit of Scatterleaf Tea?*

## Key NPC Voices
- **Nikolas Midnight:** Elderly, raspy, uses a spyglass/ear horn. Sharp and lawful.
- **Treaclewise:** Enthusiastic, chatterbox, speaks in rhymes.
- **Northwind (Treant Sapling):** Deep, slow-moving, golden-ribboned.
- **Red (Squirrel):** High-pitched, frantic, hands out dandelions.

## Mechanics Ref
- **Welcome Gifts (Hour 1):** Northwind/Red event.
    - DC 10 Dex to catch a seed.
    - Secretly collect Dandelion Wishes.
- **Bubble-Pop Teapot:**
    - DC 10 Acrobatics to steer the bubble.
    - Reward: **Scatterleaf Tea** for rhyming with Treaclewise.
- **Aris (Static Meter):**
    - High Mood: Aris is calm and hums.
    - Small Static: Near a Hag's thief or something lost.
    - Heavy Static: Near the Staff Area/Wagons.
