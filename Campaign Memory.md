# Campaign Memory: The Witchlight Veil

## Overview

- **Campaign:** The Witchlight Veil (Adapted from *The Wild Beyond the Witchlight*).
- **Style:** Social-heavy (80%), Character-driven, Emotional, Dark Comedy.
- **Session Duration:** 3-5 hours (usually 1 PM - 4/5 PM).
- **Core Theme:** Fate vs. Correction / The Veil between Desire and Reality.
- **Current Tone:** Whimsical hospitality with psychological dread; social scenes should feel warm before they become dangerous.

## Learned DM Preferences

- **Social Priority:** Prioritize dialogue, emotional beats, and complex negotiations over combat.
- **Combat as Social:** Even combat-like scenes should preserve verbal interaction, psychological stakes, and character choices.
- **NPC Depth:** NPCs should react to player backstories and emotional texture, not only plot triggers.
- **Pacing:** Allow scenes to breathe. Do not rush social scenes, memory exchanges, bargains, or quiet emotional turns.
- **Villain Style:** Hags should not be flat boss monsters. They should feel like hosts, collectors, artists, or negotiators whose cruelty has a personality.
- **Adaptation Rule:** Preserve module function, but personalize tone, clues, and emotional payoff.
- **Prep Style:** Long scene-by-scene scripted prep (readaloud text per branch, full dialogue trees) tends not to survive contact with the table — actual play regularly diverges within the first scene. Starting Session 10, prep uses a lighter "toolkit" format instead: situation snapshot, NPC agendas/motivations, open questions, and carry-over hooks, with readaloud text reserved only for the one or two beats that are genuinely guaranteed to happen.
