• Setting: The Wild Beyond the Witchlight + Homebrew Expansion
• Fokus: Roleplay, Mystery, Character Development, Feywild Exploration

• Lebih suka min-max atau optimasi build dibanding cerita
• Kurang menikmati sesi yang didominasi roleplay
