# DM Preparation: Session 1

## Pre-Session Checklist
- [ ] **Review Characters:** Read the players' character sheets, specifically focusing on their **Lost Things**.
- [ ] **Music/Ambience:**
    -   *Tavern:* Jolly, upbeat, maybe a live pan flute track for Lampiar.
    -   *The Bleeding Forest:* Mystical, slightly dissonant, "magical forest" sounds.
    -   *Pumpkin Sanctuary:* Whimsical farm ambience, light wind.
- [ ] **Maps:**
    -   Battlemap for the *Veilwood* (Forest path) in case they want to explore or if the "Bleed" manifests a combat encounter (optional).
    -   Reference map: `Tayuka` town map (if available or theater of the mind).
- [ ] **Tokens/Minis:**
    -   Aris (Blindfolded boy).
    -   Madryck (Old Mage).
    -   Generic "Fey" creatures (Pixies, Blink Dogs) just in case.

## Quick Reference

### Aris (Lunaris Thoht)
*   **Role:** Madryck's adopted son.
*   **Quirk:** Mirror Touch Synesthesia (feels what others feel).
*   **Key Line:** "Father's worry tastes like sour milk."

### The "Bleed" Mechanic
When describing the forest, emphasize the **sensory overload** before the shift.
*   **Visual:** Colors invert or become neon.
*   **Olfactory:** Smells of ozone, candy, or old books.
*   **Effect:** Don't forget to have **EVERY** player roll on the `F20 Fey Prank Table` in the Session Note.

### Madryck's Reward
Promise this to the players if they hesitate:
1.  A *Bag of Beans*.
2.  *Cloak of Displacement*.
3.  *+2 Leather Armor*.
4.  *+2 Wand of the War Mage*.
*(Note: They only get this **after** they return successful).*
