# Journey Ideas: The Road to the Carnival

After leaving Madryck Roslof's Pumpkin Farm, the party has a **3-day journey** ahead of them to reach the location where the Witchlight Carnival will appear. This journey is an opportunity to build tension, explore the "Dark Comedy" vibe, and deepen character relationships before the main event.

---

## Day 1: The Static of the Boy

**Focus:** Aris (Lunaris Thoht) and the party's "Presence."

Since Aris has **Mirror Touch Synesthesia**, he reacts physically to the party’s internal states. This makes him a living barometer for their backstories.

*   **The Weight of the Hunger (Kee Chow Ma):** Whenever Aris walks near Kee, he begins to shiver and hold his stomach. He whispers, *"It's so empty... like a hole that keeps screaming."* He is feeling the hunger of the Hexblade. 
    *   *Dark Comedy:* Aris might try to "feed" the sword a piece of his travel ration, thinking it just needs a snack.
*   **The Ticking Headache (Dr. Aurelia):** Aris avoids walking directly behind Aurelia. If asked, he says, *"You make a noise like a clock with a broken spring. It makes my teeth ache."*
*   **Narrative Beat:** A simple campsite conversation. Aris asks each character what they "smell like" (emotionally). 
    *   *Philip:* "You smell like old paper and... a small, sharp giggle hidden in a box."
    *   *Lorelei:* "You smell like autumn leaves and a song that never finishes."

---

## Day 2: The Hallucinations of Loss

**Focus:** The Carnival's "Calling" effect.

The party is getting closer to the Fey crossing. The Feywild Bleed becomes more psychological. They start seeing "lost" things or past traumas manifest in the landscape.

*   **The Scribe's Mirage (Philip):** The party finds a lonely, ink-stained desk sitting in the middle of a muddy clearing. It looks exactly like the desk Philip used to work at in the library. 
    *   *The Twist:* The drawers are filled with living cicadas that "scream" in the voices of the people Philip worked with.
*   **The Royal Echo (Lorelei):** They pass a ruin that looks momentarily like the palace Lorelei left behind. In the windows, he sees the "73 heirs" standing above him, laughing, but their mouths are sewn shut with golden thread.
*   **Dark Comedy Encounter: The Mimic-Flower.** They find a beautiful field of flowers. If Lorelei approaches (drawn by their beauty), the flowers "mimic" his face and start critiquing his performance. *"Too much vibrato, darling. You lack... soul."*

---

## Day 3: The Price of Admission

**Focus:** The Transition.

The air turns cold and reeks of ozone and popcorn. A heavy mist descends. They encounter a "Herald" before they see the lights.

*   **Encounter: The Grinning Toll-Collector.** A small fey creature (maybe a Quickling or a Redcap with a polished hat) sits on a milestone. 
    *   *The Toll:* It doesn't want gold. It wants a **"Bad Memory."** 
    *   *Interaction:* To pass the "Veil" into the Carnival's clearing, each player must describe a memory they would rather forget. The creature "eats" it (the player temporarily forgets that specific detail).
    *   *Dark Twist:* If they refuse, the creature "borrows" one of their senses for an hour (e.g., Aurelia loses her sense of time, making her feel permanently "late").

---

## Moving into Chapter 1

Once they reach the clearing, use the official read-aloud text for the Carnival Entrance, but have **Aris** go completely still. 

*"The static... it stopped. Now it's just... music. But the music sounds like it's crying."*

---

### Suggested Player Interaction Questions
1.  **Kee:** How do you explain the sword's hunger to a child who can feel it?
2.  **Lorelei:** Do you tell the others about the woman you are looking for, or do you keep her as your private muse?
3.  **Aurelia:** How do you react to a world where "logic" doesn't apply and your calculations are always wrong?
4.  **Philip:** Do you use your illusions to comfort Aris, or are you too afraid that the magic will "drift" like it did when you were a child?
