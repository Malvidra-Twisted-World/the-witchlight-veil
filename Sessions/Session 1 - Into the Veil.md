# Session 1: Into the Veil

## Synopsis
The party reunites at **The Wandering Bard Tavern** in the town of **Tayuka**, reminiscing about their shared childhood loss. A posted notice draws them to the countryside, but the journey takes a strange turn as the Feywild begins to bleed into the Material Plane. Arriving at the Pumpkin Sanctuary, they meet an old warlock, **Madryck Roslof**, and his adopted son **Aris**, setting them on a path to the **Witchlight Carnival**.

---

## Part 1: The Reunion

The adventure begins in the **Dewasentra** region, in the lively town of **Tayuka**.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> The town of Tayuka bustles with the energy of trade and travel, but inside **The Wandering Bard Tavern**, the atmosphere is warm and thick with the smell of spiced meats and sweet ale. The tavern is owned by **Lampiar**, an older Satyr with graying fur and a penchant for playing the pan flute while he pours drinks with his tail.
> 
> You find yourselves gathered at a round table near the hearth. Whether by fate or coincidence, you are all here together—faces from a shared past, united by a memory that feels like a splinter in your mind: The Witchlight Carnival, and the things you lost there as children.

**Roleplaying Beats:**
*   **Reunion:** Allow the players to introduce their characters (Level 3).
    *   **Kee Chow Ma:** The ale might taste like ash to you. Your blade vibrates slightly against your hip—a low, hungry hum. It is *bored*.
    *   **Lorelei:** (If present) The colors of the tavern seem dull compared to what you remember of the Carnival.
    *   **Dr. Aurelia:** You find yourself instinctively checking your pocket watch. The tavern's rhythm—the clinking mugs, the laughter, the bard's song—loops slightly too perfectly. It feels... calculated. *Miscalculated*.
*   **Lost Things:** Encourage them to discuss their **Lost Things** (Secrets, Smile, Direction, etc.) and how it has affected their lives.
*   **Lampiar (Satyr Innkeeper):** He is jovial but perceptive.“
    *   *To Aurelia:* "You have the face of someone listening to a clock that isn't there, Doctor."
    *   *To Kee:* "That blade of yours... it whispers louder than my flute. Keep it sheathed, friend."
    *   *To Lorelei:* "A plain cloak for such a vibrant soul? I can see the colors you *want* to wear, miss, even if you can't."

### The Call to Adventure: The Anchor

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> As the evening wears on, your eyes wander to the crowded "Jobs & Rumors" board near the entrance. Most notices are mundane—missing livestock, escort duty.
> 
> But one yellowed, crinkled paper seems to *humm* with a low vibration that only you can feel. It has been buried under newer notices, ignoring the passage of time. The seal on it is a stylized Pumpkin, but the words are written in a script that shifts as you read it.

**The Notice:**
*   **The Text:** *"The Archfey **Zybilna** has fallen silent. The Carnival returns, but the Patron is lost. Seekers of the Veil, inquire at Roslof's Pumpkin Farm."*
*   **The Trigger:** Reading the name **"Zybilna"** hits you like a physical blow.
    *   *Philip:* You remember a nursery rhyme about a "Tasha" that sounds suspiciously similar.
    *   *Aurelia:* The name feels like a missing variable in an equation.
    *   *Kee:* The sword goes instantly silent, as if *it* is listening.
*   **The Anchor:** You realize that this paper has been waiting for *you*. No one else in the tavern even glances at it.

If the players ask around about **Madryck Roslof**:
*   *DC 10 History/Investigation:* He was once a great adventurer, a warlock of some renown, but became a recluse years ago.
*   *DC 12 Rumor:* He lives on a massive pumpkin farm. People say he's "touched by the Fey" and waits for a message that never comes.

## Part 2: The Bleeding Forest

The journey to Madryck's farm takes the party through a dense stretch of wood known as the **Veilwood**.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> You leave the comfort of Tayuka and head east. The road is well-traveled at first, but as you approach the coordinates of the Roslof farm, the trees grow taller, their branches interlacing like weaving fingers. The sunlight shifts, taking on a twilight hue of lavender and gold, even though it is midday. 
> 
> The air smells suddenly of blooming night-flowers and ozone. The forest is quiet—too quiet—and then, reality seems to *hiccup*.

**The Feywild Bleed:**
The barrier between the Material Plane and the Feywild is thin here. This is a "Bleed" event. The terrain mimics the Material Plane but with vibrant, alien colors (blue grass, orange leaves, geometric clouds).

**Personal Visions (Spotlight Moments):**
*   **Kee Chow Ma:** For a fleeting moment, the trees resemble the armored silhouettes of your fallen comrades from the **Greenfield Kingdom**. They don't speak, but their silent, moss-covered armored fingers point eastward—toward the Farm. You hear a phantom clash of steel.
*   **Dr. Aurelia:** You hear it before you see it—the heartbeat of the forest. It doesn't beat like a biological heart; it *ticks*. Tock... Tock... Tock... a countdown to something.
*   **Philip:** The shadows stretch and play tricks. You see the silhouette of a child (yourself?) running through the trees, laughing. It feels like a memory manifested.
*   **Lorelei:** The flowers seem to turn and look at you. Their petals shift, forming shapes like beautiful, impossible gowns that wither the moment you try to touch them. A whisper of silk follows you on the wind.

**Event: The Fey Prank**
Ask each player to roll on the **Fey Prank Table (d20)**. These effects are temporary manifestations of the chaotic magic leaking through.

> As you push through the strange, vibrant thicket, the sensation of magic washes over you like a static shock. Then, as quickly as it began, the colors fade back to the greens and browns of the natural world. Ahead, the forest clears, revealing a sprawling farmhouse.

## Part 3: The Pumpkin Sanctuary

### Arrival
> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> You emerge from the tree line to see a farmhouse that defies explanation. The yard is filled not with crops, but with **Giant Pumpkins**, some as large as small cottages, towering nearly 50 feet high. The scent of baking pumpkin pie wafts from the main house.
> 
> A small boy, perhaps eight years old, sits on the porch. He wears simple clothes, but his eyes are covered by a thin, sheer cloth blindfold. He tilts his head as you approach, his brow furrowing in confusion.

**NPC: Aris (Lunaris Thoht)**
*   **Description:** Adopted son of Madryck. 8 years old. wears a blindfold.
*   **Condition (Mirror Touch Synesthesia):** He feels physical sensations of those he sees.
*   **Interaction:** He stands up abruptly, wary. "Who are you? Father didn't say anyone was coming... but you feel... familiar. Like static."

### Meeting Madryck
Aris hesitantly leads the party inside.

> [!NOTE] <!--easygit-callout:original=readaloud,collapse=-->
> The interior is cozy, cluttered with arcane curios and half-finished pumpkin carvings. Sitting in a rocking chair is **Madryck Roslof**. He looks frail, almost startled by the intrusion. He nearly drops his carving knife.
> 
> "By Zybilna... visitors?" He squints at you, then at the crumpled notice in your hands. "That old thing? I posted that years ago. The paper is older than the boy standing next to you." He sighs, a mix of relief and sadness. "I had given up hope that anyone would answer."

**The Quest:**
Madryck reveals the following:
*   **Zybilna:** He is a warlock, and his patron is the Archfey **Zybilna** of Prismeer.
*   **Silence:** He has not heard from her in a year. He fears something terrible has happened.
*   **The Connection:** "You... you have been touched by the Carnival before. I sense the empty spaces inside you."
    *   *To Kee:* "Your blade eats souls to fill a void. But what will fill *your* void, Sir Knight?"
    *   *To Aurelia:* "You seek to correct errors in the great design, Doctor. My patron... she *wrote* the design for Prismeer. If she is gone, the equation is unbalanced."
    *   *To Philip:* "A scholar who hides his wild heart. The Carnival misses you, Philip. It wants its trickster back."
    *   *To Lorelei:* "And you... the world feels grey to you, doesn't it? The Carnival is the only place where the colors are real enough to touch. Go, find your palette."
*   **The Ask:** Enter the Carnival, find the crossing, seek out Zybilna, and ensure she is safe.
*   **Reward:** "My inheritance. All that I have gathered in my life." (See "Madryck's Treasure" in the module).

**The Consignment:**
Before the party leaves, Madryck places a hand on Aris's shoulder.
"There is one more thing. Aris... he has been dreaming of the Carnival. He speaks of a 'Pig Lady' and a 'Golden Mirror'. I fear these are not dreams, but omens."
*   **The Request:** Madryck asks the party to take Aris with them. "He is special. He sees things we cannot. I believe he is the key to finding Zybilna."
*   **Aris's Reaction:** The boy is trembling but nods. "I have to go. The static... it's calling me."

### Conclusion
Madryck gives them directions to where the Carnival is set to appear (about 3 days travel away). The party sets off, now with a child in their care, walking toward the Veil.
