# Session 2: The Witchlight Carnival

## Synopsis
The party reaching the gates of the **Witchlight Carnival**. They navigate the entrance, barter for tickets with **Nikolas Midnight**, and witness the **Hour 1: Welcome Gifts** event. They also participate in the **Bubble-Pop Teapot** ride, all while Aris reacts to the unique sensory "static" of the carnival.

---

## Part 1: The Gates of Whimsy

The forest yields to a sprawling clearing filled with heavy, sweet-smelling mist.

> [!readaloud]
> The first stars of night twinkle above the apricot sunset. Giant dragonflies whir overhead, trailing streamers, and a low mist curls over the ground. Through a floral archway, you glimpse wondrous and vibrant creatures—elf stilt walkers, dancing faeries, and painted performers. Everywhere there is laughter, pixie dust, bubbles, and the wistful tune of a whistling calliope.

**NPC: Aris (Lunaris Thoht)**
*   **Reaction:** He stands perfectly still. He tilts his head toward the music. *"The static... it stopped. Now it's just... music. But the music sounds like it's crying."*
*   **Mechanic:** Aris's synesthesia helps the party find the "Veil" or "Lost Things." (e.g., *"There is a hollow spot here. Like someone erased a piece of the world."*)

---

## Part 2: The Ticket Booth

**NPC: Nikolas Midnight (Elderly Goblin)**
*   **Description:** Perched behind a silver-faerie-topped booth. Peers through a spyglass and uses an ear horn. 
*   **The Benefactor:** Nikolas has prepaid tickets for each character (from "an anonymous benefactor"). He hands them a map and a pair of colorful cloth butterfly wings.

### Bartering for a Ticket
If characters want extra tickets or cannot pay, they can roll on the **Ticket Pacts (d8)** table:
1.  Cannot tell a lie.
2.  Must wear a crown of flowers and water it.
3.  Perform a dance when your name is said.
4.  Can't talk about your favorite subject.
5.  Carry a small pumpkin like a precious egg.
6.  Greet all trees with reverence.
7.  Compliment everyone you meet.
8.  Declare love for unicorns.

## Part 3: Hour 1 - Welcome Gifts

> [!readaloud]
> A twelve-foot-tall walking tree (the treant sapling **Northwind**) garlanded in golden ribbons marches down the thoroughfare. Emerald clouds swirl above the tree and rain down golden, spinning **sycamore seeds**. 
> 
> A chatty squirrel named **Red** scampers along the boughs, handing dandelions to passersby. "Make a wish! Blow them away!"

**Gifts (d8 table):**
1.  **Personality Trait**: "I am easily amused" until dawn.
2.  **Ability Die**: Gain a d4 for one ability check.
3.  **Magic Gold**: The seed turns into 1 gp.
4.  **Inspiration Die**: Gain a d8 for one ability check.

**Dandelion Wishes:**
Ask each player to secretly write down a wish. (This becomes relevant in Chapter 4).

## Part 4: The Bubble-Pop Teapot

**NPC: Treaclewise (Friendly Goblin)**
*   **Quirk:** Only speaks in **Rhyming Slang**. 
    *   *"Toil and trouble"* (Bubble)
    *   *"Gnarly tree knot"* (Teapot)
    *   *"Bramble thicket"* (Ticket)
*   **The Ride**: 1 ticket punch. Occupants enter the teapot and emerge in a bubble from the spout.
*   **Mechanics**: DC 10 Acrobatics to steer. Pops after 1 minute.
*   **Reward**: If a PC speaks in rhyming slang back to Treaclewise, they get a pouch of **Scatterleaf Tea** (Protection from Evil and Good + a magical cup of tea).
