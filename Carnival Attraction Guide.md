# Carnival Attraction Guide

This guide provides a quick-prep summary of every attraction at the **Witchlight Carnival**. Each entry includes official module mechanics and specific **"Veil Hooks"** for your campaign.

---

## 1. Big Top
*   **Events**: Hour 4 (Extravaganza) and Hour 8 (Coronation).
*   **NPCs**: Mister Light (Ringmaster), performative acts.
*   **Veil Hook**: Aris feels the "Static" of the audience's collective awe. **Dr. Aurelia** might analyze the light-spells as precise geometric patterns.

## 2. Bubble-Pop Teapot
*   **Mechanic**: 1 ticket punch. DC 10 Acrobatics to steer bubble. 
*   **NPC**: Treaclewise (Goblin, Rhyming Slang).
*   **Reward**: **Scatterleaf Tea** (stand in circle for *Protection from Evil and Good*).
*   **Veil Hook**: **Philip** sees his own illusions dancing on the bubble's surface.

## 3. Carousel
*   **Mechanic**: Riddle of the 8 Unicorns (Names like Fortune/Bold). Free ride and secrets if correct.
*   **NPC**: Diana Cloppington (Human/Horse fused).
*   **Secrets**: Unicorns reveal hag weaknesses (Allergy to widdershins, Eclipse prophecy).
*   **Veil Hook**: **Lorelei** feels the carousel's rotation as a literal "Circle of Fate," echoing his muse's influence.

## 4. Dragonfly Rides
*   **Mechanic**: DC 12 Animal Handling to steer. DC 14 to save the dwarf from the panic.
*   **NPC**: Northwind (Treant Sapling), Red (Squirrel).
*   **Veil Hook**: Aris talks to Northwind about the "Static" of the sky. **Kee**'s sword hums louder the higher he flies.

## 5. Feasting Orchard
*   **Mechanic**: Faerie Cake Eating Contest (Con saves vs Custard damage).
*   **NPC**: Ellywick Tumblestrum (Planeswalker bard).
*   **Reward**: Cupcake of Invisibility.
*   **Veil Hook**: Tumblestrum recognizes the party as "Agents of Change." She might wink at **Philip** specifically.

## 6. Gondola Swans
*   **Mechanic**: Metaphysical Questions (Joy, Existence, Time). DC 10 Dex or get tipped.
*   **NPC**: Feathereen (Haughty Giant Swan).
*   **Veil Hook**: **Dr. Aurelia**'s logic vs. Feathereen's metaphysics. Aris gazes at the "Silver Mist" on the river and sees shadows of the future.

## 7. Hall of Illusions
*   **Mechanic**: Tasha's Hideous Laughter mannequin (DC 13 Wis). Mirrors show youth/age.
*   **NPC**: Candlefoot (Voice-less Mime).
*   **The Crossing**: Portal to Prismeer (Hither, Thither, Here and There...).
*   **Veil Hook**: **Philip**'s childhood reflection might actually *wave* at him. Aris feels the "Mirror-Plane" as a physical pressure.

## 8. Lost Property
*   **Mechanic**: DC 15 Perception to find the missing boy, Viro.
*   **NPC**: Dirlagraun (Displacer Beast with a toffee-juice keg).
*   **Veil Hook**: **Kee** feels the displacer beast's displacement as a "shimmer in reality." Aris asks Dirlagraun about Star (living mirror-cub).

## 9. Mystery Mine
*   **Mechanic**: All-Seeing Eye contraption. Refer to player fears. 3 failed saves = Nightmares/Exhaustion.
*   **NPC**: Zephixo (Dwarf Wizard).
*   **Veil Hook**: This is the heart of the **Dark Comedy**. **Aurelia**'s fear might be "Variable Entropy," while **Kee**'s is the "Complete Silence of the Blade."

## 10. Pixie Kingdom
*   **Mechanic**: Tiny Potion. Hide-and-Seek (Wis Stealth vs Starbug).
*   **NPC**: Jeremy Plum, Biscuit the Hamster.
*   **Reward**: Pixie Dust.
*   **Veil Hook**: **Lorelei** feels the "smallness" as a relief from the weight of his royal heritage. Aris plays hide-and-seek by "listening" to the grass.

## 11. Silversong Lake
*   **Mechanic**: Palasha's song. Heckled by Kettlesteam.
*   **NPC**: Palasha (Mermaid).
*   **Veil Hook**: **Lorelei** is captivated by the song's "Palette." Aris hears the "Singing Mirror" of the water.

## 12. Small Stalls (Games)
*   **Almiraj Ring Toss**: DC 17 Dex.
*   **Catch the Dragon**: DC 18 Perception.
*   **Gnome Poetry**: 3x DC 15 Charisma.
*   **Goblin Wrestling**: Str Athletics vs. Goblins.
*   **Outstare the Cyclops**: DC 14 Constitution.
*   **Prizes**: Trinkets, Wand of Prestidigitation, Face Paint, Spider Toy.

## 13. Snail Racing
*   **Mechanic**: DC 12 Animal Handling per round (480ft track). Initiative unnecessary.
*   **NPCs**: 8 Snails (Team Shellymoo, Quickleaf, etc.).
*   **Reward**: Potion of Advantage.
*   **Veil Hook**: **Kee** might try to "intimidate" a snail into speed using his blade's presence.
